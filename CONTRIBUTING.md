# Contributing to SentinelOS

SentinelOS is maintained by **temeboiw**. Contributions are welcome, but contribution does not automatically transfer copyright ownership.

## Required rights agreement

**Before any contribution is merged, the contributor must sign a written copyright assignment agreement approved by the project maintainer.** The agreement must explicitly assign to temeboiw the contributor's transferable copyright rights in the submitted contribution, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

A pull request, issue comment, commit, or this document alone is not a signed copyright assignment. Do not submit a contribution if you are unwilling or unable to sign the required agreement. The maintainer may decline or leave unmerged any contribution without an accepted agreement.

Until the agreement is completed and accepted, the contributor retains rights in their own original contribution. This policy does not retroactively transfer rights in code already contributed, and it cannot transfer rights in third-party code.

## What contributors must submit

1. Submit only work you created or are legally authorized to contribute.
2. Identify any third-party code, generated code, copied snippets, or other material and its applicable license.
3. Do not include secrets, credentials, private data, or code whose license is incompatible with the project.
4. Wait for the maintainer to confirm that the required rights agreement is complete before expecting a contribution to be merged.

## Project ownership and attribution

The SentinelOS project name, project history, and the maintainer's pre-existing original source remain attributed to **temeboiw**, subject to applicable law and any third-party rights. Editing existing code does not by itself make the editor the owner of the original code.

This policy does not override the existing `LICENSE`, third-party licenses, or rights that cannot legally be assigned. See `COPYRIGHT.md` for the project's attribution policy.

## Review

The maintainer decides whether a contribution is accepted. Opening a pull request does not guarantee that it will be merged.
